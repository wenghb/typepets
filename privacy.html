Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

No Data Collection

TypePets is a children's typing practice application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to any server. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms, and no ads.

Local Storage Only

All progress, settings, and profiles (up to four per browser) are stored locally on your device using your web browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Pet Passport Save Codes

A Pet Passport is a save code that your browser creates from the typing progress stored on your device, so you can back it up or move it to another browser. It is generated on your device and never sent to us. You decide where to keep it, so treat it like any other file you save.

Spoken Finger Hints

Optional spoken hints (for example, "left index finger") are read aloud by the English voices built into your device, through your browser's speech feature. Nothing is sent to us, and the hints never include personal information.

Certificates and Reports

Printable certificates and progress reports are created in your browser from the data already on your device. Printing is handled entirely by your device.

No Cookies

We do not use cookies or tracking pixels.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how our site is used. This is a privacy-first analytics tool that does not use cookies, does not track individuals, and does not collect personal information.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Fonts to display text. Google's privacy policy applies to the loading of these font files. Optional donations are handled on Stripe's website; we never see payment details. The "Share to Google Classroom" button is a plain link that only contacts Google if you click it. No other third-party services are used.

Children's Privacy

TypePets is designed for children. We are committed to protecting children's privacy. Since we collect no data whatsoever, there is no personal information at risk. Schools do not need to create student accounts or share student data to use TypePets.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the project's repository.
