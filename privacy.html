Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

No Personal Information

TypePets is a children's typing practice application. We do not collect personal information about players. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms, and no ads. Progress stays on your device unless you choose to make a save code (see below). The only other thing ever sent to us is feedback you choose to send, which includes a short summary of progress levels.

Progress Is Stored on Your Device

All progress, settings, and profiles (up to four per browser) are stored locally on your device using your web browser's localStorage. Unless you make a save code, this data never leaves your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Save Codes (Optional)

A save code is a short code such as TIGER-427-MOON that keeps one player's progress online, so it can be opened on another computer, for example when a school Chromebook clears its data. Save codes are only made when someone presses "Get my save code" on the Stats page.

What a save code stores: typing progress (lessons, levels, speed and accuracy history, and which keys were missed), the pet and the name given to it, badges, streaks, and game settings. What it never stores: the player's name (it is removed before anything is sent), email addresses, IP addresses, cookies, or device identifiers. A pet name that looks like an email address or phone number is not saved.

The code is picked at random and is not linked to who you are. Anyone who knows a code can open and change that progress, so keep it like a password. While a save code is in use, progress is saved to it automatically after each change. The online copy is used only to give the progress back to you; it is never shared, sold, or used for ads. It is stored with Cloudflare.

You can delete the online copy at any time with "Stop saving online" on the Stats page; progress stays on your device. Save codes nobody opens or uses for 12 months are deleted automatically.

Backup Files (Pet Passport)

A Pet Passport is a backup file or block of backup text that your browser creates from the progress stored on your device, so you can keep a copy or move it to another browser. It is generated on your device and never sent to us. You decide where to keep it, so treat it like any other file you save.

Spoken Finger Hints

Optional spoken hints (for example, "left index finger") are read aloud by the English voices built into your device, through your browser's speech feature. Nothing is sent to us, and the hints never include personal information.

Certificates and Reports

Printable certificates and progress reports are created in your browser from the data already on your device. Printing is handled entirely by your device.

Feedback You Choose to Send

If you use the feedback form, we receive the rating, topic and message you send. To help us fix problems, it also includes the page you were on, your browser type, screen size and language, your approximate country (provided by our host, Cloudflare), and progress levels such as how many sessions you have done and your current stage. It never includes a player's name or saved progress, and we do not store IP addresses or use cookies for it.

The form asks children not to type their name, email or address, and we automatically remove email addresses and phone numbers from messages before they are stored. A grown-up can choose to add an email address, after a quick grown-up check, if they would like a reply; we use it only to reply. Feedback is stored with Cloudflare, can only be seen by the TypePets team, and is deleted when it is no longer needed to improve TypePets.

No Cookies

We do not use cookies or tracking pixels.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how our site is used. This is a privacy-first analytics tool that does not use cookies, does not track individuals, and does not collect personal information.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Fonts to display text. Google's privacy policy applies to the loading of these font files. Optional donations are handled on Stripe's website; we never see payment details. The "Share to Google Classroom" button is a plain link that only contacts Google if you click it. No other third-party services are used.

Children's Privacy

TypePets is designed for children. We are committed to protecting children's privacy. We do not collect personal information from children: progress stays on the device unless a save code is made, save codes never include a child's name or contact details, and the optional feedback form never asks for them. Schools do not need to create student accounts or share student data to use TypePets.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, or would like feedback you sent (or a save code you can no longer open) to be deleted, please use our feedback form or contact us through the project's repository.
