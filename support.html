Hi, I'm Henry

I'm a parent, and I built TypePets for my own kids. It teaches proper finger placement with step-by-step lessons, games, real articles to type, and a pet that only grows when they practice.

I want it to stay free for every child: no ads, no accounts, and nothing to buy inside the app. Every feature is there from the first visit.

Donations cover the running costs and give me time to keep building: new lessons, more articles and new things for the pet. If TypePets is helping your family or your class, thank you for considering it.
